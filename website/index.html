VANS · FOR MAC

Ask how. It shows you, right on your screen.

VANS is a guide you summon with ⌥Space. Ask how to do something and it points at the next thing to click, in the app you are already in, and waits while you do it. When you would rather not, it can do the job for you.

TWO WAYS

Learn it once. Or hand it over.

VANS puts a ring on the next control and waits. You do the clicking, so the next time you know where it is. It moves on when the window shows you did the step. Leave the app and the guide waits for you; come back and it is on the same step.

The same steps, carried out by VANS where you can watch. It shows the plan first, and anything that changes something waits for you to confirm.

Part way through being shown, you can hand a step over. When a step is typing something in, the guide offers ⌘↵ Do it for me, and ⇧⌘↵ for the rest like it.

ASK

Start with "how do I".

CONTROL

Nothing changes until you hold the key.

It asks first.

Anything that changes a file, a setting or an app waits for you to hold Return. A press is not enough.

It can be undone.

File moves and typed text go into a record, and each can be put back.

It does not open or switch apps on its own.

If a step needs another app, opening it is a step it shows you.

You set the limits.

Places it must never touch, things it must always ask about.

PRIVACY

Plain about what leaves your Mac.

  • VANS looks at your screen only when you ask it something, and only at what the question is about.
  • To answer, it sends your request and the relevant text to a model provider, straight from your Mac. The panel tells you when it has done so.
  • What VANS remembers is kept in one folder on your Mac. You can delete it at any time.

WHY

I built VANS because I was tired of looking up how to do small things on my own Mac: where a setting lives, which menu has the thing I need. A video shows you someone else's screen. I wanted something that would point at mine and wait while I did it, so that the next time I would just know. It can also do the job for you when you do not care to learn it, and it shows you exactly what it is about to do first. VANS is early. A few people are testing it, and I would like you to be one of them.
